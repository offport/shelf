Overman and Overself

Nietzsche's Overman and Brunton's Overself in 18 illustrated points

Eighteen points, each with its own scene. Two ideas about becoming more than your everyday self: Nietzsche's Overman, reached by struggle, and Paul Brunton's Overself, reached by stillness. Use the board to jump to a point, or scroll the cards. Each opens to the idea, where it comes from, what it looks like, the trap, and a line you could build an article section on.

All 18 points

A plain-words summary of ideas from Friedrich Nietzsche (mainly Thus Spoke Zarathustra and The Gay Science) and Paul Brunton (The Quest of the Overself, The Wisdom of the Overself), written from memory with no quotations beyond a few well-known short phrases. Nietzsche never used the word Overself; pairing the two is this page's own framing. The grouping by chess piece, point 18, the "article angle" lines and all drawings are additions.
