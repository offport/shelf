Beyond Order

A guide to Jordan Peterson's 2021 book, 12 More Rules for Life

Twelve more rules. The companion to 12 Rules for Life. Where the first book warned against chaos, this one warns against too much order. Each card opens to the idea, how the book argues it, something to try, and a common objection.

Ideas that run through the book

This is a summary in plain words of ideas from Beyond Order: 12 More Rules for Life by Jordan B. Peterson (2021), not a reproduction of the book. Rule names are shortened. The "try it" lines, the objections and the drawings are this guide's own additions. The book and its author are widely debated; the objections reflect common criticisms, not a verdict.
