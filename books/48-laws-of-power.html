The 48 Laws of Power

An illustrated guide to Robert Greene's book

Forty-eight laws, each with its own scene. Use the board to jump to a law, or scroll the cards. Every law opens to the idea, the story the book tells, how it is meant to work, when it reverses, and a note on what it costs.

All 48 laws

This is a summary in plain words of ideas from The 48 Laws of Power by Robert Greene (1998), not a reproduction of the book. The grouping by chess piece, the "worth remembering" notes and all drawings are this guide's own additions. The laws describe how power has often been won, and many are plainly manipulative. The notes are there so the page can be read for recognising these moves as much as for using them.
