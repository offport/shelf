The 48 Laws of Power

A guide to the ideas in Robert Greene's book

Forty-eight laws, one square each. The six chess pieces are this guide's way of sorting them into themes. Tap a square or a row to open a law, or pick a piece to see its eight.

All 48 laws

This is a summary in plain words of ideas from The 48 Laws of Power by Robert Greene (1998), not a reproduction of the book. Law names are shortened. The grouping by chess piece and the "worth remembering" notes are this guide's own additions.
