The idea in one minute

Much of our stress comes from trying to manage other adults: what they think, how they behave, whether they include us. Robbins's answer is two short phrases said in order.

Let Them

Other people's opinions, moods and choices are not yours to control. Stop trying.

Let Me

What you think, do and say next is entirely yours. Put the energy there.

Where it came from. Robbins describes fussing over the details of her son's school prom night until her daughter told her to let them do it their way. She noticed how much lighter that felt and began applying it elsewhere. A video about it spread widely, and the book followed, written with her daughter Sawyer.
The core

Why it needs both halves

Robbins is firm that "Let Them" alone is not the theory. Used alone it turns into sulking, superiority or cutting people off. The second half is where the responsibility lies.
