The idea in one minute

Harper is a counsellor who works with trauma. Her argument is that anxiety, rage, numbness and freak-outs are not character flaws. They are a protective system doing its job too well.

1. Your brain's first priority is keeping you alive, not keeping you happy.

2. After something overwhelming, the alarm part of the brain can stay switched on and start reacting to ordinary life as if it were the original danger.

3. That wiring was learned, so it can be relearned. The thinking part of the brain can be trained to take the controls back.

The tone is deliberately blunt and full of swearing. Harper says the point is to make brain science feel like a conversation with a friend, not a lecture.
