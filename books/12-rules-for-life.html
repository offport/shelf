12 Rules for Life

A guide to Jordan Peterson's 2018 book

An antidote to chaos. Twelve rules for bringing order to a life that has too little of it. Each card opens to the idea, how the book argues it, something to try, and a common objection.

Ideas that run through the book

This is a summary in plain words of ideas from 12 Rules for Life by Jordan B. Peterson (2018), not a reproduction of the book. Rule names are shortened. The "try it" lines, the objections and the drawings are this guide's own additions. The book and its author are widely debated; the objections reflect common criticisms, not a verdict.
